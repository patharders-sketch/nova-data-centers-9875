Northern Virginia data centers: Prince William and Loudoun counties (public county GIS data, pulled Oct 15, 2026)

Download spreadsheet (.xlsx)

Sources: Prince William County GIS (gisweb.pwcva.gov) and ePortal; Loudoun County GIS (logis.loudoun.gov). Map numbers are unique across both counties. Status uses a common set: Existing/Completed, Under construction, Approved/Planned, Pending, Denied/Void. Basis type shows whether a status is stated by the county or inferred by us. PWC Digital Gateway North/South and Compass buildings are labeled Denied; the county states those rezonings were voided by the Court of Appeals on 7/29/2026 (label under review). Applicant names, MW, and Loudoun building square footage are not in the public layers. Click a number to zoom the map.
